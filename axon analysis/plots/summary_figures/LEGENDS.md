# Figure legends - RB22-24 LC axons in the superior colliculus (exploratory, 3 mice)

Conventions for every figure. **Colour = what the thing is**: signal-like axons blue, noise-like ROIs amber, rejected suite2p ROIs grey, behaviours their own hue (face motion black, whisking green, pupil magenta, ball brown, eyelid violet). **Marker shape = animal**: circle RB24, square RB23, triangle RB22. **Filled = unflagged session** (counts in pooled numbers); **hollow / dashed grey = flagged session** (drawn, never pooled). Flagged: RB23 1306 and 1704 (face / whisking / pupil tracking disagree), RB24 1527 and 1640 (6-7 min), RB24 11-06 1529, 1317, 1604 (very small behavioural range). 13 unflagged sessions: RB22 2, RB23 2, RB24 9.
**Statistics**: the session is the unit, the animal is the cluster. Pooled value = mean of the per-animal means; interval = 95% hierarchical bootstrap (animals, then sessions within animal; 4000-10000 draws). Per-session tests use circular-shift or shifted-onset nulls. With 3 mice nothing here is a population estimate; any result carried by one animal is automatically labelled ambiguous in the ledger. No multiple-comparison correction; effect sizes with intervals instead of stars.
Axon signal: mean best dF/F ((F - 0.5 Fneu - F0) / F0, F0 = running 10th percentile +-60 s) of the signal-like ROIs (V3 axon criterion) on a 5 Hz grid. Behaviour: DLC pupil / eyelid / eye position, face-pad motion energy, whisking index, ball (only where plausible). Every number can be traced in `results_ledger.csv`.
**Rejected ROIs** (the control population of Figs 2, 7, 9): every suite2p ROI of the session's own `suite2p/plane0` output that is not in the accepted set (`iscell[:, 0] == 0`; the accepted set is what was aggregated: 11-144 ROIs against 52-621 rejected per session), extracted with the same F / Fneu and the same dF/F recipe as the accepted ROIs. ROIs with a median neuropil-corrected fluorescence <= 5 counts are dropped (83-100% are kept) and at most 400 random ROIs are used per session. Control sets = 20 random subsets of the same size as the signal-like set. Not available for RB24 11-04 1640 (accept-all, 0 rejected) and 11-06 1604 (curated set contains all ROIs). The accepted labels are manual picks, not the suite2p classifier output (the classifier probability does not separate accepted from rejected in any session except RB23 1704, where the accepted set is the classifier output at p >= 0.5). Accepted ROIs have the more skewed, transient-rich traces (median skew 0.62 vs 0.31 in 1630, 0.79 vs 0.22 in 1651). Rejected therefore means "detected but not picked" (faint, noise-dominated, neuropil-like, overlapping or fragmentary ROIs), not "contains no calcium signal".

## Figure 12 - What the rejected ROIs are (is the control population free of axons?)
Added 2026-11-09. All ROIs of the 13 unflagged sessions (266 accepted signal-like, 3234 rejected), same F / Fneu and dF/F recipe. Rejected ROIs that are hidden constituents of a merged ROI (suite2p `inmerge` > 0, still `iscell` 0; 71 ROIs, almost all of them activity-like because they are pieces of accepted merged axons, e.g. 37 of the 135 activity-like rejected ROIs of 1630) are excluded. Activity-like = the rejected ROI passes the transient test used for the accepted axons (slow variance >= 4 x white-noise expectation, upward events >= 2 x downward events; applied on the 5 Hz grid, approximate: accepted ROIs pass it 40-90%). Strict control = fails the test, skewness < 0.3 and not touching an accepted ROI (1230 ROIs). **a-c** Skewness, median F / median Fneu and local contrast (mean image, ROI against a ring 2-6 px outside) of accepted signal-like, rejected activity-like and strict-control ROIs: the accepted ROIs are the most skewed, brightest relative to their neuropil and highest in contrast; the activity-like rejected ROIs lie in between and overlap with them; the strict control is flat and neuropil-like. 20% [12, 33] of the rejected ROIs (647 ROIs; mean over sessions, range 9-44%) are activity-like: possible unpicked boutons or axon fragments (the user confirmed in the labelling gallery that some rejected ROIs are axons). **d** Coupling to whisking along the activity-likeness gradient (rejected ROIs ranked by skewness within each session, 5 bins): the lowest-skew bin is ~23% significantly positive and ~65% significantly NEGATIVE, the highest-skew bin ~58% positive and ~21% negative; accepted axons 85% positive, 4% negative. **e** The same per group and session: accepted signal-like 84 [71, 93] / 7 [0, 17]%, rejected all 40 / 36%, activity-like 45 / 25%, strict control 35 [27, 44] / 44 [29, 60]% positive / negative (animal-clustered means over sessions with >= 8 ROIs): even ROIs with no calcium-like transients are coupled to whisking in about 80% of cases, with either sign. **f** The coupling does not depend on the distance to the nearest accepted ROI (touching 46 / 32%, 3-30 px 40 / 37%, 30-80 px 41 / 45%, > 80 px 43 / 45% positive / negative), i.e. it is not confined to neighbours of the accepted axons. **g** Neuropil test: with dF/F from F alone (no neuropil subtraction) the rejected ROIs are 42% positive / 43% negative, with F - 0.5 Fneu (as used) 43 / 41%, with the neuropil trace alone 34 / 27%: the sign split is not a subtraction artefact, and the neuropil itself also couples with both signs in many sessions. **h** r with whisking against F / Fneu: no relation in the rejected ROIs (Spearman -0.02); the accepted axons sit at higher F / Fneu and positive r. **i** Per-session numbers. Reading: the rejected set is a mixture of (i) unpicked axons / boutons (about a fifth; coupling like the accepted axons) and (ii) ROIs without calcium-like transients that couple to whisking with either sign; (ii) is the better control and it is not quiet, so a shared non-bouton fluctuation (brain / z motion, intrinsic optical or other absorption changes, neuropil) is present; the non-GCaMP recording is needed to separate it from real activity in out-of-plane axons. **Blind labelling galleries** (`rejected_roi_gallery/`): for RB24 10-21 1630, RB22 10-27 1138 and RB23 10-23 1642, 50 thumbnails each (20 rejected activity-like, 20 other rejected, 10 accepted, random order; mean image with ROI outline and the session dF/F trace, no behaviour), a `labels_*.csv` sheet to fill in and a `KEY_*.csv` (group of every thumbnail) that should only be opened after labelling.
In the existing galleries, thumbnails 1, 7, 8, 12 and 30 (RB24 10-21 1630) and 3, 7, 8 and 45 (RB22 10-27 1138) are hidden constituents of merged ROIs, i.e. parts of axons that are already represented by an accepted ROI (listed in `NOTE_*_hidden_merge_constituents.csv`; the label sheets were not touched). **Review queue** (`rejected_roi_review/`, built by `review_queue.py` for every aggregated session with rejected ROIs): the rejected ROIs that are not merge constituents, do not overlap an accepted ROI and are activity-like (pass the transient test or skewness > 0.5), ranked by an activity score (mean percentile rank of skewness, slow variance, local contrast, F / Fneu), at most 160 per session, as thumbnail sheets (`review_<session>.pdf`: mean image with the ROI in yellow and the accepted ROIs in cyan, session dF/F and 90 s around its largest transient; no behaviour) with a decision sheet `labels_review_<session>.csv` (accept / reject / unsure). `apply_curation.py` writes the accepted decisions into `iscell.npy` (dry run by default; backs up iscell.npy first; optional Fall.mat rebuild); the curated sessions then have to be re-aggregated and the analyses rerun.
